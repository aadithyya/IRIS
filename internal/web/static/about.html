About iris.

Less space. Same data.

IRIS is a simple, lossless file-compression tool built to make storing and sharing files more efficient.

Powered by Zstandard, IRIS compresses your files while preserving their original contents, so you can reduce file sizes without sacrificing data integrity.

We believe useful tools should be fast, simple, and focused on doing one thing well. No complicated workflows. Just upload your file, let IRIS compress it, and download the result.

Your files. Your data. Losslessly compressed.
